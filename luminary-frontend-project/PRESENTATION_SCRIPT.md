**"This sounds complicated"**

everything. But for you, you just use it like any system. 
Click Patients, see patient. Click Appointments, see 
calendar. The AI does the complex work behind the scenes."

**"Will staff need retraining?"**
→ "Minimal. Most of what staff does currently just 
disappears (good disappears). Manual NH263 management? 
Gone. Routine appointment booking? Handled by AI. What 
remains is actual patient care and complex decisions. 
Training is 2-3 hours max."

launch."

## DEMO TIPS

✓ **Stay confident** - You're showing them the future
✓ **Don't apologize** - "This is a prototype" is fine, but don't diminish it
✓ **Listen more than talk** - After each section, pause and see what they ask
✓ **Go at their pace** - If they want to drill into something, do it
✓ **Emphasize NH263** - That's the Zimbabwe-specific magic
✓ **Point to money** - "This saves you ZWL 30K/year" hits different than "This is automated"
✓ **End with a clear ask** - "Would you be interested in being a pilot partner?"
